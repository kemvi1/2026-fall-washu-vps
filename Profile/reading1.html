Restaurant Websites Are Good and We’re All Going to Miss Them

I never really thought of the vibes that someone gets from a restaurant website. I think the only times I actually go to the website is when I can’t find the menu on google maps. However, I do sort of understand the appeal of it. I think it's like going to a Chinese restaurant and having the owners' kids serve you and you then know that you are about to get a more authentic or home style meal.
