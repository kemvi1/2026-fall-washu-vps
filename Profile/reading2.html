Reading Response 2

The New Turing Test

By Elan Ullendorff — Dec 07, 2023

I thought the author brought up a pretty interesting point about how we should view AI moving forward. I think it's impossible now to go back to how things were before AI so we sort of have to just accept and adapt to what's going on around us. I have seen countless of AI images and reels on instagram but I never stoped to ask how much humanity is in each reel. I agree with the author that most people, myself included, simply put a label that something was AI generated and left it at that. I think thats because most of us think its someone who just entered a prompt and got a image or picture so we automatically assume that there was very little human effort put in. Thinking about how we could express ourselves better with AI, I think a good starting point would be prompting. Thinking about when I use AI, most of my prompts are lazy and usually a couple of words asking Chat to generate me flash cards. Another thing that we can think about is further editing after image generation.
