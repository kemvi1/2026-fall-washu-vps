Reading Response 3

My website is a shifting house next to a river of knowledge. What could yours be?

By Laurel Schwulst - May 21, 2018 

1. What did you think about the essay? Did anything in particular resonate?
    I thought the essay raised a good point that a website should be anything we want it to be. I think for me personally, I always overthink what I create should ultimately look like or what its purpose should be. But in the end, it really is whatever it needs to be or whatever you want it to be. 

2. Laurel proposes a website could be a river, a garden a thrown rock. If you have a website, what could your website be?
    I’d hope it will be more like a garden that I revisit from time to time and adjust or update. This way, I don't really feel pressured to always add things and focus more on the quality of it instead. 

3. What can you do with a website that you make yourself that you can’t do if someone else makes your website?
    I think if someone made my personal website, it would lack a lot of the personality behind it. An easy example, I feel like I’m pretty simple minded and I like to do a bunch of random stuff, so I feel like if I had a professional make my website, it might look too clean or perfect.
