Eno 

He said somewhere that feelings were the start of thoughts. I thought this was pretty interesting and it really did make me wonder which actually comes first. It's easy for me to come up with examples where feelings come first such as I feel sad but I but not sure why. The feeling comes first before the understanding and thought of why I am sad is realized. However, there are few examples where I do think a thought that comes up with me just thinking and it might make me laugh or feel excited. But is that because I was already in a good mood which led me to those thoughts? Honestly not sure, but I do think it was something interesting he said.

Another thing that I thought was really interesting about Eno’s workflow was that he was often very inspired by things outside of music. For example, he would compare a beat to a camel and the audience would understand because of the camel's hump. Or when he said the song sounded like a lumberjack and wanted to go towards a more 9-5 route. I thought this was pretty interesting because it sort of goes back to his analogy of music being like painting but with sounds. It really does help me understand how Eno thinks and tries to put things he experiences around him and represent it in the form of music.
